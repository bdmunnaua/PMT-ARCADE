1. Summary

PMT Arcade (pmtarcade.com) is a free browser games platform, built independently in Bangladesh. Anyone can play 16 quick games without a download or a purchase, climb the leaderboards, take part in a weekly tournament, and earn PMT for their scores, daily check-ins, invites and original content about the platform.

PMT is the platform's token: a standard BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain with a fixed supply of 10,000,000,000 PMT. Its contract has no owner, no mint function and no tax. The aim is wide distribution: many players each holding PMT they earned by playing, rather than a few large holders.

The platform is financed by advertising on the free games and by small fees. It does not depend on selling PMT.

2. The platform

16 free games

Neon Rush, Merge 2048, Tower Stack, Snake Arena, Learn English and more. Free to play in any browser, on phone or computer.

Leaderboards & tournament

Weekly leaderboards for every game, and one featured tournament game each week with PMT prizes for the best scores.

Play with friends

Ludo, Chess, Carrom, Call Bridge and 29: invite friends with a link by WhatsApp, Messenger, Facebook or imo, or play against a clearly marked 🤖 bot.

Creator rewards

Players who make original content about PMT Arcade can receive a creator reward after review.

Players sign in with Google or email. Every account gets a player number, and every PMT movement is recorded in a double-entry ledger that cannot be edited afterwards. The site is available in English and Bangla.

4. Creator rewards

Campaign #1 rewards original content about PMT Arcade with 100,000 PMT per approved creator, for the first 1,000 approved creators — at most 100,000,000 PMT, or 1% of the supply.

  • Sign in and finish at least one game first.
  • Make your own Reel, video or post (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) that shows pmtarcade.com.
  • Say clearly that you receive a PMT reward, using the platform's “Paid partnership” label where available.
  • Submit the link on the Creator rewards page. The team checks every post.
  • Rewards are for original content only — never for likes, follows or shares. One reward per person and per social account; multiple accounts are rejected.

6. Withdrawing to your wallet

Players can move eligible PMT to their own wallet on BNB Smart Chain (for example MetaMask or Trust Wallet). The current minimum is 100,000 PMT and the network fee is 5,000 PMT per withdrawal. Each withdrawal is checked and sent by the team, usually within 48 hours, from a separate payout wallet that holds only a small amount. PMT can also be deposited back from your wallet.

9. Market price and reference rate

PMT does not have an established external DEX market price yet. PMT is not listed on any exchange.

Buying and selling PMT on the platform are paused. Any internal ৳ rate shown on PMT Arcade is a platform reference only and must not be read as the external market value of PMT.

10. Liquidity

Liquidity is planned for a later phase. If and when PMT Arcade adds a liquidity pool on a decentralised exchange, it follows these rules:

  • It is funded only with money that is not owed to players — platform income such as advertising.
  • The pool's LP tokens are locked for at least 12 months with a public locker, linked on the transparency page.
  • The team share stays in its published wallet, or is locked, for at least 12 months after a listing.
  • A listing happens only after legal review, and is announced with these rules — never with a price target.

11. Transparency

The transparency page shows live figures from the platform's ledger and from BNB Smart Chain, with totals only and no personal data: whether every PMT is accounted for, the result of the last full ledger check, PMT given out and left in the rewards pool, and every published project wallet with its live on-chain balance.

12. Fair play and security

  • The server decides every game result, reward and balance. Nothing the browser says is trusted.
  • Free-game scores are checked for impossible speed and length; suspicious runs pay nothing and are flagged.
  • Bots are always shown as 🤖 bots, use the same dice as everyone and never see hidden information.
  • Every PMT movement is a balanced double-entry record that cannot be edited, checked by a built-in integrity check.
  • Each reward, withdrawal and deposit can only be processed once.
  • PMT Arcade will never ask for your password, bKash PIN, OTP or wallet secret phrase.

13. Roadmap

Each phase starts when the one before is working. There are no fixed dates.

  1. Phase 1 — now: free games, PMT rewards, tournaments, play with friends, creator campaign, referrals.
  2. Phase 2: grow towards 10,000 genuine players; measure who comes back and what the platform earns.
  3. Phase 3: more uses for PMT inside PMT Arcade, smoother withdrawals and wider distribution.
  4. Phase 4: legal and token documentation, and the liquidity structure.
  5. Phase 5: DEX liquidity under the rules in section 10. Only then does an external market form for PMT.

14. Risks and legal notice

  • This whitepaper explains how PMT and PMT Arcade work. It is not an offer to sell PMT and makes no promise about any price, value or return.
  • PMT has no external market today. Whether a market forms later, and at what price, is not known and is not promised.
  • Laws on digital tokens and online games differ by country. Features such as withdrawals, buying, selling or games with stakes may be paused or changed to follow the law in each country.
  • Blockchain transactions cannot be undone. Sending PMT to a wrong address or network loses it.
  • Play responsibly. Players under 18 must not take part in games with stakes.
